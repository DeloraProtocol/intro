Privacy Notice

Last updated: June 1, 2026

Welcome. This Privacy Notice explains how Delora Labs Inc. (“Delora,” “Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and otherwise processes personal information in connection with our websites, including delora.build and any websites or subdomains that we own or operate and that link to this Privacy Notice (collectively, the “Sites”), and the related APIs, developer tools, Partner Portal, widgets, documentation, integrations, products, and other services offered by us (collectively, the “Services”).

This Privacy Notice does not address our privacy practices relating to Delora employees, contractors, or other personnel.

Blockchain participants

Please note that, by virtue of the public nature of blockchains, the holdings, transactions, and other activity associated with a cryptocurrency wallet address may be publicly available and accessible to third parties.

This may include, but is not limited to, public wallet addresses, sender and receiver addresses, token addresses, token amounts, transaction hashes, smart contract interactions, calldata, events, routing or bridge-related information, and any other data included in or associated with a blockchain transaction.

Information stored on a blockchain may not be capable of being modified or deleted due to the immutable nature of blockchain networks. Transactions, addresses, and related blockchain activity may reveal information about a user’s identity and may be correlated, now or in the future, by any party who chooses to do so, including block explorers, RPC providers, analytics providers, indexers, data providers, and other blockchain participants.

Please consider how privacy and transparency on blockchains work before using any blockchain-based service.

Our collection of personal information

We collect personal information in connection with your visits to and use of the Services. This includes information you provide to us, information from third parties, and information collected automatically, including through cookies, local storage, and similar technologies.

Personal information collected from individuals

We may collect the following personal information submitted to us by individuals through the Services:

Account information. To use certain Services, including the Partner Portal or developer tools, you may be required to create an account. This may involve collecting your email address, username, company name, website, social or messaging handles, public blockchain addresses, authentication credentials such as hashed passwords, account settings, and related account activity. We use this information to administer your account, provide the Services, communicate with you, authenticate access, and provide customer support.

Integration and developer information. If you use our APIs, Partner Portal, widget, developer tools, or integration features, we may collect information related to your integration, such as integrator names or identifiers, display names, API access credentials, fee settings, rate limit settings, public blockchain addresses used for fee collection, and related configuration or usage information. We use this information to provide, secure, operate, support, and improve the Services.

Token, wallet, quote, route, and transaction information. If you use or interact with the Services, we may receive certain token, wallet, quote, route, swap, bridge, transaction, and event-related information. This may include public blockchain addresses associated with self-hosted wallets, wallet types, chain IDs, token addresses, token amounts, token balances, transaction hashes, transaction status, smart contract information, route or bridge-related information, metadata describing a transaction, and other transaction or event-related information. We use this information to provide quotes, routes, transaction data, execution-related functionality, analytics, security, debugging, and support.

Communications. If you communicate with us through a form, email, social media, support channel, or other method, we may collect your name, email address, company information, message content, and any other personal information you choose to provide. We use this information to investigate and respond to your inquiries, communicate with you, provide support, improve our Services, and manage our business.

Marketing communications. If you sign up to receive newsletters, updates, announcements, or other marketing communications, we may collect your email address and related communication preferences. You may stop receiving promotional email messages from us by clicking the unsubscribe link provided in those communications or by contacting us. You may not be able to opt out of service-related communications, such as account verification, transactional messages, technical notices, security notices, or legal updates.

Personal information automatically collected

As is true of many digital platforms, we and our third-party providers may automatically collect personal information from your device, browser, and usage of the Services. This may include:

  • IP address;
  • browser type and version;
  • browser identifier;
  • device type;
  • operating system;
  • URLs visited;
  • referring pages or campaigns;
  • date and time of access;
  • time spent using the Services;
  • usage activity and analytics data;
  • general location derived from IP address;
  • API request metadata;
  • errors, logs, performance data, and diagnostic information;
  • cookies, local storage objects, pixels, and similar technologies.

We use this information to operate the Services, understand usage, improve performance, prevent abuse, debug issues, enforce rate limits, maintain security, and improve user experience.

Cookies and similar technologies

We may use cookies, local storage objects, pixels, and similar technologies to operate and improve the Services, remember preferences, understand usage, perform analytics, and support security.

You can usually manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking or deleting cookies may negatively impact your experience using the Services, as some features may not work properly or at all.

Emails we send may contain tracking technologies, where enabled, such as pixels or tracked links, to help us understand whether messages are opened or links are clicked. You can often block these technologies by changing your email client settings to disable remote images or external content.

Personal information from third parties

We may receive personal information from third parties, including:

Business partners and integrators. We may receive information from companies, developers, applications, wallets, protocols, or other partners that use or integrate the Services.

Wallets and wallet providers. When you connect or interact with a wallet, we may receive information such as your public wallet address, wallet type, chain, token balances, and related transaction and technical information.

Blockchain networks and public sources. We may collect or derive information from public blockchains, block explorers, RPC providers, blockchain analytics providers, data providers, and other public or third-party sources.

Social media and community platforms. If you interact with us on platforms such as X, Telegram, Discord, or similar services, we may receive information based on your privacy settings and the information you choose to share.

Legal claims. We may process personal data where necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Data retention

We retain personal data for no longer than reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Notice, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by applicable law.

The criteria used to determine retention periods include the type of personal data, the purposes for which it is processed, the legal basis for processing, applicable legal requirements, security needs, dispute resolution needs, and our legitimate business interests.

Where we process personal data based on contract, we generally retain it for the duration of the contractual relationship and for an additional limited period where needed to comply with law or address legal claims that could arise from the relationship.

Where we process personal data based on consent, we generally retain it until consent is withdrawn or until the data is no longer needed for the purpose for which consent was provided.

Where we process personal data based on a legal obligation, we retain it for the period necessary to fulfill that legal obligation.

Where necessary, we may retain personal data for longer to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, enforce our agreements, or comply with legal process.
