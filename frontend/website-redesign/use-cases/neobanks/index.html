Questions?
We’ve got answers.

Does Delora provide bank accounts or fiat conversion?

Delora provides infrastructure for moving and converting digital assets. Banking services and fiat conversion require your own providers or partners.

Who authorizes a transaction?

Your business or customer authorizes the transaction through their wallet. Delora does not hold their private keys.

What can we build with Delora?

Use Delora to add supported stablecoin deposits, transfers and conversions to your product.

Can we track transactions?

You can track supported transactions through your integration or the partner portal.
