Questions?
We’ve got answers.

What does Delora do?

Delora helps businesses move and convert stablecoins across networks through one API. Build deposits, payouts and liquidity flows into your product.

Which stablecoins and networks do you support?

Delora supports stablecoins including USDC and USDT across networks such as Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum and Solana. Explore the documentation or talk to our team about the assets and networks you need.

How long does a transfer take?

Based on our data, transfers between networks such as Base, Arbitrum and Solana take 2–3 seconds on average.

What does Delora cost?

Delora is free to integrate. We charge a flexible fee in basis points on transaction volume, with custom pricing available. Learn more on our pricing page.

Does Delora hold our funds?

Delora is non-custodial and does not hold your private keys. Your business or users authorize transactions from their own wallets.

How long does integration take?

Integration usually takes around one day. Our team can help you get started and answer questions along the way.

What happens if a transaction is delayed or fails?

You can check the transaction status and contact our team with the transaction details for help.

How do I get started?

Follow our getting started guide to integrate the API or widget, or talk to our team about your use case.
